BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Preparing for your Institute is as easy as 1-2-3.

Complete these actions in the Attendee Hub, then build a schedule around the conversations that matter most to you.

01. Submit Your Travel Details

Please submit your Travel Details through the “Book Hotel” tab of your Attendee Hub to make your hotel reservation for the event.

02. Create Your Attendee Profile

Prior to the event, please complete the brief questionnaire addressing your specific operational goals and strategic objectives for the program.

03. Create Your Itinerary

Customize your event agenda by selecting your conference sessions, Think Tanks, and business meeting preferences for the Institute.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Preparing for your Institute is as easy as 1-2-3.

Complete these Sponsor Hub deliverables so your onsite team and meeting strategy are ready well before arrival.

01. Submit Your Delegate Profiling Question, Company Details & Logo

Submit your company profile information and one Delegate Profiling Question for inclusion in the delegate profile questionnaire. These details help attendees better understand your organization, products, and services, while the questionnaire responses provide valuable insights into delegate priorities and challenges to support more informed and productive business meeting selections.

02. Register Onsite Attendees & Submit Hotel Needs

Please identify all individuals from your organization, including presenters, who will be attending the event, and submit their information via the Register Onsite Attendees section of the Sponsor Hub. Your Operations Lead will add them to the event roster and coordinate their hotel accommodations.

03. One-on-One Meeting Selections

Review the Delegate Profile Summaries and identify your top preferences for your pre-scheduled one-on-one business meetings.
