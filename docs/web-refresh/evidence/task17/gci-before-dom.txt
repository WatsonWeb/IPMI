- heading "INSTITUTE FOCUS" [level=3]
- paragraph:
  - strong: "Strategy-Focused, Peer-Driven:"
  - text: Navigating Regulatory, Legal, and Operational Complexity in Healthcare
  - strong: .
- paragraph: Healthcare stands at a critical inflection point as the convergence of regulatory, financial, and technological forces continues to influence and reshape the industry.
- paragraph: Amid ongoing policy shifts and evolving enforcement priorities, health system leaders are operating in an environment defined by heightened scrutiny, constrained resources, and increasing expectations. Providers, payers, and other stakeholders are being compelled to adapt in real time as they confront financial pressures, workforce challenges, changing care delivery models, and growing demands for access, transparency, and value.
- paragraph: At the same time, the pace of innovation is accelerating. Advances in artificial intelligence, data analytics, virtual care, and value-based arrangements are transforming how healthcare is delivered and managed. While these developments present meaningful opportunities, they also introduce complex legal, compliance, and operational risks that require thoughtful oversight and governance. The resulting landscape demands new approaches to risk management, enterprise decision-making, and cross-functional alignment.
- paragraph: "Within this dynamic environment, the Healthcare Law & Compliance Institute will convene leading General Counsel, Chief Legal and Compliance Officers, and industry experts to address these challenges and opportunities. Centered on the theme “Strategy Focused, Peer Driven: Navigating Regulatory, Legal, and Operational Complexity in Healthcare,” the Fall 2026 Healthcare Law & Compliance Institute, hosted at the Park Hyatt Aviara, offers a comprehensive forum for advancing legal excellence, strengthening compliance, and shaping the future of healthcare leadership."
- paragraph: Through panels, interactive think tanks, and collaborative roundtables, participants will engage in candid dialogue, share practical insights, and explore actionable strategies to address today’s most pressing challenges. We look forward to welcoming you this Fall for a highly engaging and collegial experience. Click on the Agenda to learn more.
