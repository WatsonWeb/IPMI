## How to maintain this register

Each task worker must record every placeholder it actually applies, including the exact text or asset, Designer element or CMS record/field, staging URL, prior value, and required final replacement. Distinguish planned values below from installed content. Do not overwrite an existing verified event date, quote, staff contact, or approved asset merely to demonstrate a placeholder.

Use a visible `Placeholder` or `Pending confirmation` label for invented facts. Use non-sending preview states for unknown recipients and external destinations; do not route messages to guessed addresses or turn placeholder URLs into live CTAs. For missing photos, an existing appropriate site asset may be retained temporarily and identified by its actual asset URL in the applied register. Do not attribute fabricated quotations to real people.
